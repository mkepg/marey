The reference is the formal description of the language: every keyword, every property and the rules that make a scene replay identically.

- [The Marey language](/reference/language/) describes what each construct does, in what units, and what happens at its edges. It is the repository's `docs/LANGUAGE.md`, rendered, and its examples run on request.
- [Properties](/reference/properties/) lists every property of every object kind with its value, default, constraint and whether it can be animated. It is generated from the compiler's property contract.
- [Determinism in Marey](/reference/determinism/) explains why a scene replays identically, how that is checked, and the one place it is not guaranteed. It is the repository's `docs/determinism.md`, rendered.

If you are new to Marey, start with [Learn](/learn/). The [guides](/guides/) cover installing and exporting.
